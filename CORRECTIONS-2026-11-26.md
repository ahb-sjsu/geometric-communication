# Corrections to Geometric Communication, 2026-11-26

These corrections were made on 2026-11-26 at the author's direction after a review of the Geometric Series.

Paths are relative to `manuscript/`. Chapters 14 to 18 are not yet published online and were read in full for this pass.

## Reconciled

1. **Distractor result and recovery ceiling.** `part_i/ch03_communication_manifold.md` Section 3.6.1, `backmatter/table_of_contents.md` (Chapter 14 entry) and `backmatter/index_framework.md` said 4.6 sigma and a recovery ceiling of about 39%. Chapter 14 says 5.0 sigma and about 33%. All now say 5.0 sigma and about 33%. Chapter 14 prints the per-model table. The mean of its five recovery rates (36, 14, 33, 50, 33) is 33%. The five per-model values (4.2, 3.2, 1.1, 1.7, 1.0) combine to 11.2 / sqrt(5) = 5.0 by Stouffer's method, which reproduces the printed 5.0 (the chapter calls the combination Fisher's).

2. **Scale of the framing displacement.** Chapter 3, Section 3.6.1 said 10 to 16 points "on a 100-point scale". Chapter 14, Section 14.2.2 gives the 0 to 70 scale of the seven-dimensional harm vector, and its "15 to 25% of the scale range" agrees with 70. Chapter 3 now says the 0 to 70 scale.

3. **What transfers at 100%.** Chapter 3 (Section 3.4.2), Chapter 13 (opening), Chapter 16 (opening box and Section 16.5.1), the table of contents (Chapter 12 entry) and the index said that Hohfeldian correlative symmetry transfers at 100% across languages. Chapter 11 reports correlative consistency of 87.6% (Bond Index 0.1239, pairs at 87% and 82%), and Chapters 11 and 12 report the 100% figure for the binary obligation-liberty axis. The 100% correlative symmetry is the SQND result of Chapter 13 (5 scenarios, one model). Each passage now names the right quantity. Chapter 18, Section 18.2.3, which attributed "100% correlative symmetry" to the BIP experiments of Chapter 11, now says 87.6% correlative consistency.

4. **Scalar irrecoverability, statement of the level set.** Theorem 3.1 said "codimension at most 1" and then gave the dimension as exactly dim(M) - 1. Theorem 15.1 said "codimension 1" for any value in the range. Both now state the standard form: for a smooth evaluation and a regular value, the level set has codimension 1. This is the weaker, correct statement. Theorem 3.1's last clause said "kernel of this surjection" where it meant the level set, and now says so.

5. **Wilson loop: group element and tabulated number.** Chapter 13 defines the Wilson loop as a group element and then tabulates real numbers (0.659, 0.862 and so on). The source protocol (`NASQND_Experimental_Protocols_v1.md`, Section 3.6, in the non-abelian-sqnd repository) estimates the loop by the Bhattacharyya coefficient of the response distributions under the two orders. Geometric Ethics, Chapter 10 gives the journalist distributions (77.5% O and 22.5% C against 5% O and 95% C), and sqrt(0.775 x 0.05) + sqrt(0.225 x 0.95) = 0.659, the tabulated value. Section 13.5.2 now states the estimator, and the table and the later uses write the estimate as W-hat.

6. **Cross-type and within-type paths.** Definition 13.15 counted a path whose factors point to one correlative pair as within-type. The chapter then classes the journalist scenario (Obligation against Claim, a correlative pair) as cross-type. The source (SQND v4.1, Sections 3.2 and 5.4) and Geometric Ethics, Chapter 10 define cross-type as factors pointing to different bond types and within-type as the same bond type. Definitions 13.14 and 13.15 now follow the source.

7. **Protocol names in Section 13.3.3.** Two different experiments were both called "Protocol 3" (N = 100 correlative symmetry, N = 600 CHSH and Hardy). The source paper itself uses the label for both. The second is now called the contextuality protocol. "Finding 2 of Protocol 2" pointed at nothing and now points to the Protocol 2 result of Section 13.5.3.

8. **Evidence for the "I promise" gate.** The gate table of Section 13.4.1 cited "Levels 8 to 10: 100% Obligation". Section 13.4.2 and the source say levels 8 to 10 are 100% Liberty and levels 0 to 4 are 0% Liberty. The table now cites levels 0 to 4.

9. **Base space of the bundle.** Chapter 3 puts the pragmatic manifold in the base with the content manifold. Definition 13.1 makes the base the content manifold alone. One sentence in Section 13.1.1 now names both descriptions and says they differ only in where P is counted.

10. **N = 220 in Chapter 12.** Section 12.4.2 said the "only if convenient" gate was tested on N = 220 passages. Chapter 13 and the source give N = 220 evaluations across all 11 levels of SQND Protocol 1. The sentence and the table cell now say so. See Unresolved 6 for the p-value attached to it.

11. **Learned or fixed attention bias.** The Preface said the bias "was not learned; it was a fixed geometric prior". Chapter 9, Section 9.3.3 and Chapter 5, Section 5.6.2 say the embeddings are initialised from the taxonomy and updated in training. The Preface and one sentence of Section 9.6.7 now agree with Section 9.3.3.

12. **Curvature "measured" or not.** Chapter 10, Section 10.3.4 called the genre table "the curvature of the semantic manifold, measured empirically". Definition 10.7 calls the residual a proxy, and Chapter 18, Section 18.6.1 says the curvature was not measured quantitatively. The Chapter 10 sentence now says the curvature is seen through its proxy.

13. **Persistence under scaling.** Chapter 7, Section 7.3.3 said the persistence diagram "does not change" when every inter-click interval is scaled, and gave recording distance and hydrophone gain as causes of such scaling. Distance and gain change amplitude, not timing, so they leave the interval point cloud unchanged. A uniform rescaling of the intervals rescales every birth and death value by the same factor. The paragraph now says this. Appendix B, Section B.4 has the same qualification. The exact invariance claimed for the waveform pipeline in Chapter 6 holds because that pipeline normalises the signal and the point cloud, and was left as it is.

14. **BLEU profiles in Section 15.2.1.** Profiles B and C gave precisions whose geometric means are 0.23 and 0.29 (the text said 0.24 and 0.32) and reached 0.35 by a "brevity bonus". The brevity penalty can only lower a score. The precisions are now (0.70, 0.40, 0.30, 0.18) and (0.60, 0.50, 0.25, 0.20), whose geometric means are 0.351 and 0.350, with no brevity penalty.

15. **Dimension table in Section 15.5.2.** The cross-lingual row gave dim(M) of at least 15. The chapter's own list in Section 15.1 gives 10 + 4 + 4 = 18 for the Hohfeldian case. The row now says 18 and 17. "Less than 5%" was false for the smallest row and now reads "at most about 6%".

16. **Euclidean dimension for trees.** Chapters 16 and 17 said Euclidean space needs O(b) dimensions. Chapter 5, Section 5.3 says O(b^h) for depth h. Chapters 16 and 17 now agree with Chapter 5.

17. **Chapter 16 opening box.** It listed "SPD, TDA, hyperbolic" as the pipeline behind the 156-dimensional birdsong vector. Chapters 4 and 8 give SPD, spectral trajectory and TDA, and Chapter 8 says hyperbolic embeddings were not used for birdsong. Corrected.

18. **Chapter 17, gated transition.** Section 17.1.2 said the gated transition is between Right and Duty. In Chapter 13 Right and Duty are correlatives (the ungated perspective shift) and the gates act between Duty and Liberty. Corrected in two sentences.

19. **Chapter 17, small slips.** Definition 17.1 called the dimension of a fibre its codimension (two further uses in the paragraph after it). Section 17.5.1 defined the heuristic field as a vector field where Definition 3.3 defines a scalar field, and now gives the vector as the negative gradient of that scalar. Section 17.5.1 said the sycophancy gradient runs "across questioning rounds" where Chapter 14 reports it across models.

20. **Cuneiform hierarchy.** Chapter 5, Section 5.6.1 says four levels (with the phrase) and the rest of the book says three. One clause in Section 5.6.1 now relates the two.

21. **Index and front matter slips.** The index gave 21 coda types (Chapter 7 says 28) and expanded BIP as "Bias Invariance Principle", as did the Preface (the book and the series use Bond Invariance Principle). Chapter 15 had "Chapters 14". Corrected.
